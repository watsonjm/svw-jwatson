# Shoal Point Charters policies and FAQ

Shoal Point Charters runs half-day fishing charters for walleye and perch on
Saginaw Bay, Lake Huron, from Port Calloway, Michigan.

## Policies

1. **Trips and prices.** Half-day trips (5 hours) only. $300 for the first person
   plus $50 for each extra person, up to 4 people total. Kids count toward the 4.
2. **Deposit.** A $200 deposit is due when you book. The rest is paid at the dock.
3. **Your cancellation.** Cancel 14 or more days before the trip for a full deposit
   refund. With less than 14 days' notice the deposit is kept, but with 7 or more
   days' notice you can move the trip to a new date once.
4. **Weather.** Only the captain cancels for weather, by 8 PM the night before. If
   the captain cancels, you get a full refund or a free reschedule. If the trip is
   on and you don't show up, you lose the deposit.
5. **Weather during a trip.** If the captain ends a trip early for safety within the
   first 2 hours, you get a free reschedule. After 2 hours the trip counts as
   complete.
6. **Licenses.** Everyone 17 or older who fishes needs their own Michigan fishing
   license, bought before the trip. The captain checks licenses at the dock.
7. **Catch limits.** Michigan daily limits apply to each licensed angler. Limits
   can't be pooled or kept for someone who isn't fishing. Fish over the limit are
   released.
8. **Fish cleaning.** The crew fillets and bags your catch for $1.50 per fish.
   Bring a cooler with ice.
9. **Kids.** Kids 6 and older are welcome. Kids under 13 wear a life jacket at all
   times.
10. **Alcohol.** Canned beer and seltzer only, for guests 21 and older. No glass and
    no liquor. The captain can end the trip for drunkenness, with no refund.
11. **Seasickness.** No refunds for seasickness. Take medication before the trip.
12. **Payment and privacy.** Never give card numbers in chat: pay by the payment
    link we text you or at the dock. Bookings are only discussed with the person who
    booked, after they confirm the phone number on the booking.
13. **Gift certificates.** Valid for 1 year from purchase. Not refundable.
14. **Fishing rules.** Limits, size limits, and seasons are set by the Michigan DNR,
    and the crew follows them on board. We don't give legal advice; check the
    Michigan DNR fishing guide.

## FAQ

- **When do we meet?** Morning trips meet at the dock at 5:30 AM, afternoon trips
  at 12:30 PM.
- **What's provided?** Rods, tackle, and bait. Bring food, drinks, sunscreen, and
  rain gear.
- **Is there a bathroom?** Yes, there's a toilet on board.
- **Where do I park?** Free parking at the marina.
- **Should we tip?** Tipping the mate 15-20% in cash is customary.
- **How do I reach you?** bookings@shoalpointcharters.example or (989) 555-0187,
  7 AM-7 PM daily, May through October.
