EMBER 384 LRF - USER MANUAL (firmware 2.4)
Fictional company and products created for an ADK training exercise.

BUTTONS (top of unit, front to back)
- Power: hold 3 seconds to turn on or off. Tap to put the screen to sleep.
- M (menu): tap to open the quick menu, hold 2 seconds for the full menu.
- Up / Zoom: tap to step digital zoom (1.5x, 3x, 6x, 12x).
- Down / Palette: tap to change color palette.
- R (range): tap for a single range reading, hold for continuous scan mode.
- Camera: tap for a photo, hold 2 seconds to start or stop video.

COLOR PALETTES
- White Hot (default): warm objects appear white. Best all-round choice.
- Black Hot: warm objects appear black. Easier on the eyes in long sessions.
- Red Hot: the hottest 10 percent of the scene is shown in red.
- Ember: orange-to-yellow palette, good in fog and humid air.
- Outline: edges only. Useful for spotting movement in thick brush.

IMAGE MODES (full menu > Image > Scene)
- Forest: higher contrast, for brush and timber.
- Open: lower noise, for fields and long distance.
- Rain/Fog: boosts contrast and slows the refresh to reduce speckle.

LASER RANGEFINDER
- Single reading: tap R. The distance shows for 8 seconds.
- Scan mode: hold R. Readings update twice per second for 30 seconds.
- Units: full menu > Rangefinder > Units (yards or meters).
- The laser is Class 1 and eye-safe. Do not aim it at aircraft.
- If readings look wrong, run the boresight check in the Calibration guide.

HOG HIGHLIGHT (added in firmware 2.3)
Full menu > Assist > Hog Highlight. Draws a box around low, wide heat
signatures that move. It is a visual aid only and does not identify species.

RECORDING
- Photos and video save to internal storage (32 GB).
- Audio recording: full menu > Recording > Audio (on/off).
- Pre-record buffer: keeps the 10 seconds before you press record.

POWER
- One 18650 cell, about 5 hours. Battery icon flashes at 10 percent.
- Auto sleep after 5 minutes without input (full menu > System > Sleep).
