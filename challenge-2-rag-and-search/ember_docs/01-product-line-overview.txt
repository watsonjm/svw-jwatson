EMBER OPTICS - PRODUCT LINE OVERVIEW (2026)
Fictional company and products created for an ADK training exercise.

Ember Optics designs handheld thermal imagers and laser rangefinders at its
workshop in Emberline, Montana. Every product is assembled and repaired in
Emberline.

MODELS

Ember 256 (entry level, $899)
- Thermal sensor: 256x192, 12 micron, NETD under 35 mK
- Lens: 13 mm, base magnification 1.0x, digital zoom to 4x
- Detection range: up to 700 yards (deer-sized target)
- No laser rangefinder, no Wi-Fi
- Battery: built-in, about 7 hours, charges over USB-C
- Weatherproofing: IP65
- Best for: tracking, property checks, first thermal

Ember 384 LRF (flagship handheld, $1,399)
- Thermal sensor: 384x288, 12 micron, NETD under 18 mK
- Lens: 15 mm, base magnification 1.5x, digital zoom to 12x
- Laser rangefinder: up to 1,200 yards, accuracy +/- 1 yard
- Detection range: up to 1,500 yards
- Wi-Fi with the EmberLink app, photo and video with audio, 32 GB storage
- Battery: one removable 18650 cell, about 5 hours
- Weatherproofing: IP66
- Best for: hog and predator hunting at night, scanning open country

Ember 640 LRF (professional, $3,299)
- Thermal sensor: 640x512, 12 micron, NETD under 20 mK
- Lens: 35 mm, base magnification 2.5x, digital zoom to 20x
- Laser rangefinder: up to 1,500 yards
- Detection range: up to 2,600 yards
- Wi-Fi with the EmberLink app, 64 GB storage, built-in compass
- Battery: two removable 18650 cells, about 8 hours
- Weatherproofing: IP67
- Best for: search and rescue, long-range scanning, guides

Ember Range 1500 (laser rangefinder, $449)
- 6x optical magnification, no thermal sensor
- Range: 1,500 yards reflective, 800 yards on deer
- Angle-compensated ranging for bow and rifle modes
- Battery: CR2, about 4,000 readings
- Best for: daytime ranging, pairing with a thermal for night setups

WHICH ONE SHOULD I BUY?
- Under $1,000 and mostly within 300 yards: Ember 256.
- Night hunting where you need range and a clear image: Ember 384 LRF.
- Long distance, professional use, or all-night battery: Ember 640 LRF.
- You already own a thermal without a rangefinder: add the Ember Range 1500.
