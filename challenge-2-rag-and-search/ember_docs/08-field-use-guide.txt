EMBER OPTICS - FIELD USE GUIDE
Fictional company and products created for an ADK training exercise.
Recommended settings from the Ember Optics field team in Emberline, Montana.
Always follow your state's hunting regulations, including any limits on
thermal devices and night hunting.

HOG HUNTING (Ember 384 LRF recommended)
- Palette: White Hot for scanning, Red Hot to pick out the hottest animal.
- Scene mode: Open for fields, Forest along creek bottoms.
- Turn on Hog Highlight (firmware 2.3 or later).
- Range the group with scan mode (hold R) before moving closer.
- Keep the wind in your face; thermal does not hide your scent.

COYOTES AND PREDATORS
- Palette: Black Hot reduces eye strain during long sits.
- Zoom: scan at 1.5x, check movement at 6x.
- Use the 10-second pre-record buffer to capture the approach.

SEARCH AND RESCUE (Ember 640 LRF recommended)
- Palette: White Hot; scene mode: Open.
- Use the compass (E11 if not calibrated) to report bearings with ranges.
- Run two 18650 cells plus an EP-10 power bank for all-night searches.

FOG, RAIN, AND HUMID NIGHTS
- Thermal range drops in heavy fog and rain, often by half.
- Use the Ember palette and Rain/Fog scene mode (firmware 2.3 or later).
- Expect rangefinder "no return" (E8) when ranging through heavy rain.

COLD WEATHER (below 10 F)
- Battery life drops by about 40 percent.
- Keep a spare cell warm in an inside pocket to avoid E1.
- Let the unit adjust to outside air for 5 minutes before scanning to stop
  the image from drifting.
