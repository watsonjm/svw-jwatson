EMBERLINK APP GUIDE
Fictional company and products created for an ADK training exercise.
Works with Ember 384 LRF and Ember 640 LRF. The Ember 256 has no Wi-Fi.
Available for iOS 17 and later and Android 12 and later.

PAIRING
1. On the unit: full menu > Wi-Fi > On. The network name is EMBER-XXXX,
   where XXXX is the last four digits of the serial number.
2. The default Wi-Fi password is ember + the last four digits of the serial
   number (for example ember4821). Change it in full menu > Wi-Fi > Password.
3. Join the network from your phone's Wi-Fi settings, then open EmberLink.
4. The unit appears under Devices. Tap it to connect.

LIVE VIEW
- Streams the thermal image to the phone at 30 frames per second within
  about 30 feet.
- A second person can watch the stream while you scan.
- Tap the phone screen to take a photo or start recording on the phone.

FILES
- Gallery shows photos and videos stored on the unit.
- Download copies files to the phone. Delete removes them from the unit
  (use this to clear error E5).

FIRMWARE UPDATES
- EmberLink checks for new firmware each time it connects.
- Tap Update, keep the phone within 10 feet, and keep the unit battery above
  50 percent. An update takes about 4 minutes.

GPS TAGGING
With firmware 2.2 or later, photos taken while the app is connected store
the phone's GPS location.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Unit not listed: make sure the phone is joined to the EMBER network, not
  home Wi-Fi. Turn off "auto-switch to mobile data" on Android.
- Stream freezes: move closer, or lower stream quality in Settings > Stream.
- Wi-Fi error E9: update to firmware 2.4.
