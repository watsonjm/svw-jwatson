EMBER OPTICS - CALIBRATION AND MAINTENANCE GUIDE
Fictional company and products created for an ADK training exercise.

SHUTTER CALIBRATION (all models)
The unit clicks every few minutes to recalibrate the sensor. This is normal.
To calibrate manually, tap Power and Up together. Manual calibration fixes
most grainy or uneven images. Choose the mode in full menu > Image >
Calibration: Auto (default), Manual, or Silent (no click, for close-range
observation; image quality drops after about 10 minutes).

RANGEFINDER BORESIGHT CHECK (Ember 384 LRF and Ember 640 LRF)
Use this if readings are wrong or error E7 appears.
1. Pick a flat target 100 to 200 yards away, such as a barn wall or sign.
2. Rest the unit on a steady surface.
3. Open full menu > Rangefinder > Boresight.
4. Move the on-screen cross with Up and Down (vertical) and Zoom held
   (horizontal) until it sits on the spot the laser hits.
5. Press R three times to take test readings. All three should agree within
   2 yards.
6. Press M to save. Boresight settings survive firmware updates.

LENS CARE
- Thermal lenses are germanium, not glass. Never use glass cleaner.
- Blow off grit first, then wipe with the included microfiber cloth and the
  Ember lens pen.
- Keep the lens cap on in daylight; direct sun can damage the sensor (E4).

BATTERY CARE (18650 models)
- Use protected, button-top 18650 cells rated 3,000 mAh or higher.
- Ember sells a matched 3,500 mAh cell (part EB-18650) and a two-bay charger
  (part EC-2).
- For storage longer than a month, remove the battery and keep it at about
  half charge.
- In cold weather, carry a spare cell in an inside pocket (see error E1).

SERVICE
Ember Optics recommends a factory check every two years. Units are serviced
at the Emberline, Montana workshop with a typical turnaround of 10 business
days.
