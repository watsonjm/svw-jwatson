EMBER OPTICS - ACCESSORIES AND COMPATIBILITY
Fictional company and products created for an ADK training exercise.

BATTERIES AND CHARGING
- EB-18650 battery (3,500 mAh, $24): fits Ember 384 LRF and Ember 640 LRF.
  The 640 LRF takes two cells.
- EC-2 two-bay charger ($39): charges EB-18650 and other protected 18650s.
- The Ember 256 has a built-in battery. It cannot use 18650 cells and charges
  only over USB-C.
- Ember Range 1500 uses a CR2 lithium cell.
- EP-10 USB-C power bank ($59): runs any thermal model in the field; adds
  about 10 hours to a 384 LRF.

MOUNTS AND CASES
- ET-1 tripod adapter ($29): 1/4-20 thread for all thermal models.
- EH-384 hard case ($49): fits Ember 384 LRF with two spare batteries.
- EH-640 hard case ($59): fits Ember 640 LRF.
- EC-S soft chest harness ($69): fits all handheld models.
- Ember thermals are handheld only. They cannot be mounted on a rifle.

LENS AND CARE
- Lens pen and microfiber kit (EL-K, $15).
- Rubber eyecup replacement: EE-1 (256 and 384), EE-2 (640).
