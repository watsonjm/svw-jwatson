PLOWING PRIORITIES AND CLEARING TARGETS - Alaska Department of Snow (fictional agency)
Document ADS-POL-001. Effective 2026-10-01.
Fictional agency and policy created for an ADK training exercise.

PURPOSE
This policy sets which roads the Alaska Department of Snow (ADS) plows first,
how much snow triggers plowing, and how fast each road must be cleared.

ROAD PRIORITY CLASSES
Every road segment ADS maintains has one priority class.
- P1: highways and life-safety routes (hospital, airport, emergency access).
- P2: arterials and main collectors.
- P3: residential, rural, and seasonal roads.

PLOWING TRIGGERS
Plowing starts when new snow on the road reaches the trigger depth.
- P1: plowing starts at 2 inches of snow.
- P2: plowing starts at 4 inches of snow.
- P3: plowing starts at 6 inches of snow.

CLEARING TARGETS
The clock starts when snowfall ends.
- P1: cleared within 4 hours after snowfall ends.
- P2: cleared within 12 hours after snowfall ends.
- P3: cleared within 24 hours after snowfall ends.

ORDER OF WORK
- Always clear P1 before P2, and P2 before P3.
- A plow is not sent to a P2 road while a P1 road in its region still needs
  plowing, and not to a P3 road while a P2 road still needs plowing.
- A road that has not reached its trigger depth does not hold up work on a
  lower class. It simply does not need plowing yet.
- Sanding and ice treatment follow the same P1, P2, P3 order.

SUMMARY TABLE
Class  Roads                                 Start at   Cleared within
P1     highways and life-safety routes       2 in.      4 hours
P2     arterials and main collectors         4 in.      12 hours
P3     residential, rural, seasonal roads    6 in.      24 hours

WORKED EXAMPLE
A storm leaves 5 inches of new snow and snowfall ends at 3:00 PM.
- P1 roads passed the 2 inch trigger, so they are plowed first and must be
  clear by 7:00 PM (4 hours).
- P2 roads passed the 4 inch trigger. They are plowed after P1 and must be
  clear by 3:00 AM the next day (12 hours).
- P3 roads are under the 6 inch trigger, so plowing has not started on them.
