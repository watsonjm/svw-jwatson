STORM LEVELS, CONTACTS, AND DATA USE - Alaska Department of Snow (fictional agency)
Document ADS-POL-006. Effective 2026-10-01.
Fictional agency and policy created for an ADK training exercise.

ABOUT ADS
The Alaska Department of Snow (ADS) coordinates plow dispatch, road closures,
and public safety alerts during winter storms. Headquarters and the Operations
Center are in Anchorage. ADS has four regions: Anchorage, Mat-Su, Kenai, and
Fairbanks. Each region has a Regional Duty Supervisor.

STORM LEVELS
ADS gives each winter storm a level based on the forecast.
- Level 1: under 6 inches of snow expected.
- Level 2: 6 to 12 inches of snow expected, or winds of 35 mph or more.
- Level 3: over 12 inches of snow expected, or a blizzard warning.

WHAT HAPPENS AT LEVEL 3
- The Emergency Operations Center (EOC) activates.
- All available plows go on duty.

CURRENT NAMED STORM
Storm Kodiak is a Level 2 storm.

WORKED EXAMPLE
- Forecast of 4 inches of snow with light wind: Level 1.
- Forecast of 4 inches of snow with 40 mph winds: Level 2, because winds are
  35 mph or more.
- Forecast of 9 inches of snow: Level 2.
- Forecast of 14 inches of snow, or any blizzard warning: Level 3. The EOC
  activates and all available plows go on duty.

DATA AND AI USE
- No personal information about crash victims or callers in any alert or log.
  This includes names, phone numbers, addresses, and license plates.
- Staff may only use ADS-approved AI tools.
- Consumer AI tools are prohibited for ADS work.
- AI tools assist staff. Decisions that policy gives to a Regional Duty
  Supervisor, such as closing or reopening a road, stay with the supervisor.
