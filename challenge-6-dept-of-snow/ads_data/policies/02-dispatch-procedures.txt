DISPATCH PROCEDURES - Alaska Department of Snow (fictional agency)
Document ADS-POL-002. Effective 2026-10-01.
Fictional agency and policy created for an ADK training exercise.

PURPOSE
This procedure tells dispatchers at the Alaska Department of Snow (ADS) how to
choose and assign a plow to a road segment.

REGIONS
ADS has four regions: Anchorage, Mat-Su, Kenai, and Fairbanks. Each region has
a Regional Duty Supervisor. Every plow has a home depot in one region.

WHICH PLOW TO SEND
- Assign the closest available plow in the same region as the road.
- A plow that is already assigned is not available.
- A plow in maintenance can't be dispatched, even if it is the closest one.
- Check the operator's hours on shift before assigning (see ADS-POL-003, Crew
  Safety and Hours). If the closest plow's operator cannot take the job, use
  the next closest available plow in the region.

BORROWING A PLOW FROM ANOTHER REGION
- Use plows from the road's own region first.
- Borrowing a plow from another region needs approval from both Regional Duty
  Supervisors: the supervisor of the region lending the plow and the
  supervisor of the region receiving it.
- Without both approvals, the plow stays in its home region.

TANDEM PLOWING IN WHITEOUT CONDITIONS
During whiteout conditions, P1 highways are plowed in tandem (two plows). The
dispatcher assigns two plows to the segment, and they work it together.

ROAD PRIORITY ORDER
Dispatch follows the road priority classes.
- P1: highways and life-safety routes (hospital, airport, emergency access).
- P2: arterials and main collectors.
- P3: residential, rural, and seasonal roads.
Always clear P1 before P2, and P2 before P3.

DISPATCH STEPS
1. Confirm the road segment, its region, and its priority class.
2. List the plows in that region and remove any that are assigned or in
   maintenance.
3. Pick the closest available plow. Check the operator's hours on shift.
4. If the road is a P1 highway in whiteout conditions, assign a second plow
   for tandem plowing.
5. If the region has no suitable plow, ask both Regional Duty Supervisors to
   approve borrowing one from another region.
6. Record the assignment in the dispatch log.

WORKED EXAMPLE
A P1 highway in Mat-Su needs plowing during whiteout conditions. Mat-Su has
one available plow, and its other nearby plow is in maintenance. The
dispatcher assigns the available Mat-Su plow. Tandem plowing needs a second
plow, and the plow in maintenance can't be dispatched. The dispatcher asks to
borrow an available plow from Anchorage. The borrowed plow is assigned only
after both the Mat-Su and the Anchorage Regional Duty Supervisors approve.
