PUBLIC ALERT STANDARDS - Alaska Department of Snow (fictional agency)
Document ADS-POL-005. Effective 2026-10-01.
Fictional agency and policy created for an ADK training exercise.

PURPOSE
This standard covers every public road alert the Alaska Department of Snow
(ADS) publishes: web, SMS, and social media.

ALERT LEVELS
ADS uses three alert levels.
- Advisory: the road is open, and drivers should use caution.
- Warning: travel is hazardous or restricted.
- Closure: the road is closed.

REQUIRED CONTENT
Every alert names:
1. the road
2. the segment or mileposts
3. the reason
4. the detour, if any
5. the next update time

WRITING RULES
- Use plain language. No agency jargon or internal codes.
- The SMS/social version is at most 280 characters.
- Never speculate about injuries, victims, or causes of crashes. Say only
  what is confirmed about the road, for example "crash blocking both lanes".
- No personal information about crash victims or callers in any alert.

APPROVAL
- Closure alerts need Regional Duty Supervisor approval before publishing.
- A drafted Closure alert is held until the supervisor approves it.

UPDATES
- Active alerts are updated at least every 2 hours.
- Every update gives a new next update time.
- When the road returns to normal, publish a final update and end the alert.

WORKED EXAMPLE (training sample, not a live alert)
SMS/social version of a Closure alert, approved by the Regional Duty
Supervisor before publishing:

ADS CLOSURE: Dalton Highway is closed from MP 10 to MP 25. Reason: visibility under 1/4 mile in blowing snow. Detour: none available. Next update: 2:00 PM. Road info: call 511 or visit ads511.example

The sample names the road, the mileposts, the reason, the detour, and the
next update time. It is 199 characters, under the 280 character limit.

PUBLIC CONTACTS
Public road info: 511 or ads511.example. Media questions: media@ads.example.
